\section*{Prespecification and Reproducibility}

Prior to any execution, a protocol document specifying the claims to be tested,
the statistical test for each claim, the gate criteria, and the
multiplicity-correction family was finalized and cryptographically checksummed.
That document is dated 2026-08-04 and the earliest canonical run artifact is
dated 2026-08-05, so the plan precedes the results it governs. All statistical
analyses reported here follow it, and deviations arising during execution were
recorded as dated amendments rather than applied silently.

We state the limits of that evidence rather than leave them to be inferred. The
protocol was not lodged with a third-party registry before execution, so its
priority rests on file timestamps and checksums under our own control rather
than on an independent record. Depositing it now would make the document
readable but would not establish its timing, and we do not present a post hoc
deposit as though it did. Readers who require independent temporal evidence
should treat the plan as prespecified and internally documented rather than as
externally registered.

Result artifacts were frozen on completion, with per-file checksums recorded
in a manifest, so that every number reported in this manuscript is traceable
to an immutable file.

\section*{Data and Code Availability}

Both datasets are publicly available from their original sources: ISIC
2019~\cite{tschandl2018ham10000,hernandezperez2024bcn20000,codella2018isbi} and
PAD-UFES-20~\cite{pacheco2020padufes}. Neither is redistributed here. The
frozen backbone is the released PanDerm ViT-L/16
checkpoint~\cite{yan2025panderm}, used without modification and pinned by
SHA-256 in the artifact manifest; it is obtained from its authors rather than
from us.

The per-checkpoint matrix underlying the comparison in
Section~\ref{sec:results} is published with the study it comes
from~\cite{tran2026decodable}, so the figure this paper cites can be recomputed
independently rather than read off a plot.

Analysis code, the trained adapters for all three arms and five seeds, the
extracted representations, and the per-file checksum manifest are held under
version control with the frozen artifacts they describe, and are available from
the authors on request pending deposit at submission. We state availability
this way because the deposit has not yet been made; nothing here is promised
that does not exist.
