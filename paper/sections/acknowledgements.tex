\section*{Acknowledgment}

The authors thank the organizers and contributors of the ISIC
2019~\cite{tschandl2018ham10000,hernandezperez2024bcn20000,codella2018isbi}
and PAD-UFES-20~\cite{pacheco2020padufes} datasets, both of which are
publicly available, and the authors of the PanDerm model~\cite{yan2025panderm}
for releasing pretrained weights.

\section*{Preregistration and Reproducibility}

Prior to any execution, a protocol document specifying the claims to be
tested, the statistical test for each claim, the gate criteria, and the
multiplicity-correction family was finalized and cryptographically
checksummed. This document was not deposited with a public preregistration
registry; the record is internal, timestamped, and available from the authors
on request. All statistical analyses reported here follow that document, and
deviations arising during execution were recorded as dated amendments rather
than applied silently.

Result artifacts were frozen on completion, with per-file checksums recorded
in a manifest, so that every number reported in this manuscript is traceable
to an immutable file.
