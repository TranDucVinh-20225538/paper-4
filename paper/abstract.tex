Distance-based estimators are widely used to flag domain-shifted inputs before
a clinical model acts on them, and representation geometry has been reported to
track how well they perform across frozen foundation-model backbones. Reading
that association as an explanation requires a step correlational comparison
cannot supply: the geometry a metric measures must be geometry the scorer
actually reads. We make that requirement precise and then evaluate it. We show
that the spectral-tempering operator used to match geometry sets the condition
number in closed form, $\kappa(\Sigma')=\kappa(\Sigma)^{1-\gamma}$, while
leaving Mahalanobis scoring exactly invariant once its parameters are refit; for
that estimator the geometry-matched comparison therefore has no difference
available to detect, and its null is entailed by the algebra rather than
measured. Cosine, $k$-nearest-neighbour, and kernel-density scoring are not
invariant and remain admissible. Empirically, we report a preregistered
within-backbone intervention study on a frozen dermatology foundation model
(PanDerm ViT-L/16), with ISIC 2019 in-distribution and PAD-UFES-20
domain-shifted, neither in the pretraining corpus. Backbone, feature layer,
splits, and scorer definitions are held fixed while the representation is
reshaped along a dose ladder against a matched-budget control, with
implementation-integrity gating, an explicit manipulation check, and
multiplicity correction fixed before execution. Domain information and
in-distribution task performance persisted after intervention, and the targeted
geometry moved measurably, so the absence of a relationship is not an artefact
of a destroyed representation. The probe--reliability dissociation reproduced in
direction but roughly two orders of magnitude smaller than originally reported
(mean AUROC gap $\approx$0.002 versus $\approx$0.3), with all estimators above
0.99 on the unmodified representation. We therefore do not report a test of the
explanatory account. We report the boundary of where it can be tested: a regime
in which every estimator already exceeds 0.99 leaves this design little room to
observe an account operating, and in which one of the four estimators cannot
respond to the manipulation at all. This locates what such an evaluation
requires, and it is a claim about what the present study can support, not about
what holds in regimes it did not evaluate.
