how bad, and what the fallback is. Written at the end of Phase 0 (Scientific Positioning), before
any experiment exists, so these are risks to the *project*, not bugs in a *protocol*.

## How this file relates to the others

`03_Novelty_Threats.md` tracks *whether someone already answered this*. `09_Risk_Register.md`
tracks *what could still go wrong even if no one did*. Together with `Scientific_Hypothesis.md`
(the falsifiable claim) and `08_Mechanism_Map.md` (the candidate explanations), this closes
Phase 0. Nothing here is a Step 6 protocol — no datasets, models, or metrics are being committed
to; this is a pre-mortem, not a plan.
